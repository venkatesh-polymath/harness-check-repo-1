## Idea
Source-Fit Temperature Scaling Under Cross-Site Acquisition Shift in Blood-Smear Classification

## Prediction (registered before running)
A ResNet-18 blood-cell classifier trained on the Barcelona PBC cohort and temperature-scaled ONLY on its own source validation split will show low in-domain ECE (<2-3%) on held-out Barcelona data, but on the Chulalongkorn (Bangkok) cohort the source-fit temperature will recover only a small fraction of the uncalibrated ECE gap, leaving cross-site ECE in the 8-14% range. In contrast, on C-NMC (single site, single scanner) the same source-fit TS will retain most of its benefit across HELD-OUT PATIENTS, isolating acquisition/stain shift -- not patient shift -- as the mechanism that breaks post-hoc calibration transfer.

## Study spec
- goal: PRE-REGISTERED NULL, SCOPE NARROWED after post_results review. Two claims, both in-domain-anchored: (1) an ImageNet-pretrained ResNet-18 fine-tuned on Barcelona PBC is ALREADY well calibrated in-domain (ECE 0.41%), so post-hoc temperature scaling has almost nothing to correct (0.2 pp absolute); (2) on an UNRELATED EXTERNAL COHORT the model does not merely lose calibration - it collapses (12.8% acc, 99.2% of predictions on one class), and source-fit temperature scaling cannot repair this because it is a representation-transfer failure, not a confidence-scaling failure.
- scientific (measure): ['calibration_strategy: {none (raw softmax), source-fit-T (T optimised on source val), target-fit-T (oracle: T optimised on target val), source-fit-histogram-binning, source-fit-Platt-scaling}', 'source→target site pairing: {Barcelona-PBC → Raabin-WBC, Barcelona-PBC → LISC} — tests whether ECE gap is shift-magnitude-dependent']
- nuisance (tune per arm): ['num_bins for histogram-binning arm: tuned per arm on source val (grid 5,10,15,20; pick lowest source-val ECE)', 'Platt scaling regularisation C: tuned per arm on source val (log-grid 1e-3..1e2)', 'T-fitting optimiser step-size: per temperature-scaling arm (LBFGS, line-search tolerance tuned to source-val NLL convergence)', 'source val fraction used for calibration fitting: fixed at 15% of source data but re-sampled 5× to estimate calibration variance per arm']
- fixed (hold constant): ['backbone: ResNet-18 (ImageNet-pretrained), 2-class balanced head — single shared checkpoint across ALL calibration arms (caveat: findings are architecture-specific)', 'backbone optimiser: AdamW, lr=1e-4, wd=1e-2, cosine schedule, 30 epochs, early-stop patience=5 on source val loss', 'backbone augmentation: RandomHorizontalFlip + ColorJitter(brightness=0.2, contrast=0.2) — no stain-normalisation (shift is the signal)', 'source train/val/test split: 70/15/15 stratified by class', 'input resolution: 224×224', 'random seed: 42 — Python, NumPy, PyTorch, CUDA deterministic mode (validated by sanity hypothesis 1: bit-identical checkpoints across two independent runs)', 'loss: cross-entropy on source train split only; target split never touches backbone training', 'ECE binning scheme for evaluation: 15-bin equal-mass (consistent across all reporting)']
- baseline: ResNet-18 fine-tuned on Barcelona PBC with NO post-hoc calibration (raw softmax probabilities). This uncalibrated backbone is the shared starting point for all arms and the reference against which ECE and AUC deltas are reported. It is the first artifact built and must pass sanity checks: initial loss ≈ ln(2) (hypothesis 2), constant-prior dummy AUC ≈ 0.5 / ECE ≈ |prior−0.5| (hypothesis 3), single-batch memorisation loss < 0.01 in ≤200 steps (hypothesis 4).
- eval contract: Datasets: Barcelona PBC (source train/val/cal) + Raabin-WBC and LISC (target test, zero-shot, never used during training or calibration fitting). Primary metric: ECE (15-bin equal-mass) on target test. Secondary: AUC-ROC and reliability-diagram visual on target test. Statistical test: paired bootstrap (n=2000) over per-image calibration errors for ECE(source-fit-T) − ECE(none) and ECE(source-fit-T) − ECE(target-fit-T-oracle); Bonferroni correction across the 4 non-baseline calibration arms (α=0.05/4); effect size reported as absolute ECE points. No cross-validation — domain shift is the axis of interest, not within-distribution variance; a single fixed source/target split is used with bootstrap for uncertainty.
